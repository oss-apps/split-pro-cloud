SplitPro is free with no daily limit on expenses. Receipt photos, categories, recurring expenses and a default split per group are included, with no paid tier. It is open source under the MIT license and works in any browser.

Settle Up has a free version and a paid Premium upgrade, which is where its receipt photos, categories, recurring transactions, Excel export and charts are. It runs on Android, iPhone and the web.

_Last checked October 14, 2026, against Settle Up's own website and app store listings. SplitPro is our app._

## Where SplitPro is stronger

- Receipt photos, categories and recurring expenses are free.
- A default split per group, so new expenses start the way your group usually splits.
- One account for all your groups and one-to-one balances with friends.
- Imports your friends and groups, with balances, from a Splitwise backup.
- Open source under the MIT license, and you can host it yourself.

## Where Settle Up is stronger

- Native Android and iPhone apps, with offline entry.
- Several payers on one expense, and items from a bill.
- Works out the fewest transfers to settle a group.
- Converts between currencies with real-time exchange rates.

## Which should you pick?

**Pick SplitPro if** you want receipts, categories and recurring costs like rent without paying for an upgrade, or you prefer open source software.

**Pick Settle Up if** you need offline entry, several payers on one expense, currency conversion or charts.

## Common questions

### Is Settle Up free?

It has a free version. Premium adds receipt photos, categories, recurring transactions, Excel export and charts, as a subscription or a one-time purchase for one group.

### Does SplitPro work offline?

No. SplitPro needs a connection to add expenses.

### Which handles multiple currencies better?

Settle Up converts with real-time exchange rates. SplitPro supports more than 100 currencies and keeps each expense and balance in the currency it was paid in, without converting.

### Can friends see balances without installing an app?

Yes, in both. Settle Up can share a group as a link. SplitPro runs in the browser, and quick split shares a link that needs no account.
